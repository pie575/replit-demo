# Cloud Aug 2026 Pricing Updates

Starting **August 1, 2026**, Replit Cloud prices drop across all plans. The new prices apply automatically to all of your published projects at the start of your first billing cycle that begins on or after August 1, 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

### When do the new prices take effect?

The new prices go live on **August 1, 2026**. Your account switches to the new prices at the start of your first billing cycle that begins on or after August 1, 2026. Because billing cycles renew throughout the month, the exact date depends on when your subscription renews.

### Which plans get the new prices?

All plans: Replit Core, Replit Pro, Teams, and Enterprise.

### Do I need to change anything in my app?

No. The new prices apply automatically. Your deployments, databases, and storage keep running without any action from you.

### Where can I see my current usage and charges?

Visit the [usage page](https://replit.com/usage) to track your consumption and costs in real time. See [Managing Your Spend](/billing/managing-spend) to set spending limits and budgets.
