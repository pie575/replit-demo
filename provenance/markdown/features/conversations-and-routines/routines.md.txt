# Routines

> Schedule recurring work from a Conversation and receive the results in the same thread.

A Routine schedules recurring work from a Conversation and returns each result
to that same thread.

Continue to **[Run routine tasks](/chat/routines)** for availability, limits,
creation, and management steps.
